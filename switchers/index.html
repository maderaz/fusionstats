How a switch is matched
Every Fusion vault deposit and withdrawal is read, leaving out synthetic flows (wrappers and rebalances). For each wallet, each deposit is paired with one earlier withdrawal from a different vault, by the same wallet: the closest in size within the amount match, then the most recent, and each withdrawal funds one deposit at most. In is the deposit's USD value; Match the smaller of the two amounts over the larger, with the withdrawal beneath it; Gap the time between them. The in and out links open the two transactions on their networks' explorers.
