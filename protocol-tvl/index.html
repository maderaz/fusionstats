Methodology
Each vault's TVL is read on-chain once a day (tvl-snapshots.json: its total assets at that day's block, at the asset's price that day) and summed by network into tvl-daily.json by tools/build-tvl-daily.js. A vault's last reading carries forward over a day it wasn't read. Today's point is every vault's TVL as IPOR's API reports it now, refreshed with each update: the same figure as Key Metrics'. Vaults with no daily readings yet (on HyperEVM and Katana, some $15K in all) are held at today's TVL across the whole history, so the chart starts where the first deposits lift the total above that floor. By network stacks the three largest networks today and puts the rest together.
