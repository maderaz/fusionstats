What is counted
Every deposit into a Fusion vault, at its USD value when it was made, leaving out synthetic flows (wrappers and rebalances). A dust deposit is one under the chosen size; Share of all deposits sets them against every deposit with a USD value. A bar is a day, a week or a month; a click on one narrows the breakdown to it. Unticking a vault takes it out of the chart and the figures.
