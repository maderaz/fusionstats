Method: per-chain TVL is anchored on the right edge to the on-chain snapshot in ipor-vaults.json (sum of tvl across vaults, at the file's updatedAt timestamp) and walked backward by daily net flow (deposits minus withdrawals in USD at event time, synthetic flow excluded). It approximates TVL change over the tracked event window; older points do not include yield accrual or deposits made before our event indexer started, and can diverge from the historical on-chain TVL for that reason. Negative values clamped to zero.
