Methodology
Each vault's TVL is its real on-chain totalAssets() measured at every snapshot block (tvl-snapshots.json) and forward-filled between snapshots, summed per chain per day. Snapshots above a per-vault sanity bound (over 50× the vault's own median, or ≥ $2B) are dropped as decimals/price parse errors. Vaults the snapshot indexer hasn't reached yet (small, newer) are held flat at their ipor-vaults.json API value. The right edge equals the current Total TVL above. Negative values clamped to zero.
