Methodology
Read on-chain from the Aave V4 spoke the stock vaults use on Base (0x1790…7b8d, their AAVE_V4 market's substrate), by collect-aave-v4.js every six hours. Aave V4 total is everything supplied to a reserve (getReserveSuppliedAssets); Fusion supplied is the sum over Fusion's stock vaults (getUserSuppliedAssets); Fusion USDC debt is what those vaults owe at the spoke's USDC reserve (getUserTotalDebt), shown against the stock each vault holds. Prices are the spoke's own oracle's (getReservePrice). Idle is stock sitting in the vaults themselves (balanceOf), counted nowhere above. The deposit cap is the hub's addCap for this spoke. The chart is a reading a day, at each day's last block, priced that day.
