How people got in
Two kinds of contract bring money in: one that pushes a deposit on your behalf — a zap, an aggregator, a referral wrapper — and one that deposits for itself and holds the shares for its own depositors, which is what an autocompounder does. Both are counted here. Each is named from Basescan; anything we cannot name keeps its address and says why.
