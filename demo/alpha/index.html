What an Alpha
can and cannot do.

The role is narrow on purpose: wide enough to run a strategy, never wide enough to take the vault.

Can

  • ✓Send fuse actions, batched in one PlasmaVault.execute
  • ✓Supply, borrow, swap, loop and claim on granted markets
  • ✓Rebalance as often as the strategy needs
  • ✓Act atomically: every step lands, or none does

Cannot

  • ✕Touch a market the vault has not granted
  • ✕Send the vault's assets to itself
  • ✕Change fees, roles or grants
  • ✕Get past the Guardian's emergency controls

From signal
to block.

How one step of a strategy reaches the chain.

01Read

Rates, prices and the vault's positions, from the chain and anywhere else.

02Decide

Strategy logic off-chain, in any language; the Python SDK helps.

03Act

The step becomes fuse actions, sent in one execute call.

04Check

Fuses check each grant; balance fuses read the new NAV.

Strategies Alphas
run today.

A few of the shapes Fusion vaults take.

Rebalancing

Move supply to wherever the rate is best, across the granted markets.

Looping

Supply, borrow and supply again to multiply a yield, within set limits.

Carry

Hold an asset that pays and fund it with one that costs less.
