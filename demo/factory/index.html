From idea to vault
in four steps.

Every choice is made once, at deploy, and read by anyone afterwards.

01Pick the asset

One underlying asset per vault, on any network Fusion runs on.

02Grant markets

Choose the fuses and the markets in them the vault may ever touch.

03Set the rules

Roles, fees, withdrawal window and limits, in plain parameters.

04Deploy

One transaction: the vault and its managers, live and ERC-4626.

What you
configure.

The factory asks for these. Nothing else is needed to go live.

Underlying asset
One ERC-20 per vault, deposited and withdrawn through the ERC-4626 standard.
Fuses and markets
Which protocols the vault may use, and which markets in each (its substrates).
Roles
Owner, Atomist, Alpha and Guardian, each with only the powers its role needs.
Fees
Management and performance fees, alongside the DAO fee package.
Withdrawals
The withdraw window, and the instant path users redeem through.
Limits
A cap on the vault's size and limits per market.

Built to be
trusted.

What holds for every vault the factory deploys.

Permissionless

No allowlist and no sign-up: anyone with a wallet can deploy a vault.

Bounded by design

A vault only ever acts on the markets it was granted, whoever runs it.

Readable from block one

Its assets, fuses, roles and fees are onchain the moment it exists.
