Plasma Vaults

ERC-4626 vaults that hold the assets. Each is isolated, with siloed risk: deposit once, reach every integrated market.

Fuses

Composable, non-upgradable connectors to other protocols. Supply, borrow, swap, loop: each action is a fuse.

Alpha Engines

Strategy logic runs off-chain and acts onchain within approved parameters: rebalancing, carry, looping.

Guardians & Roles
