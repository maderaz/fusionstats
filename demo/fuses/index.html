How Aave V4 is built

Liquidity in Hubs. Risk in Spokes.

Liquidity lives in Hubs; users act on Spokes, each with its own risk parameters, oracle and reserve list. A Spoke reserve is an append-only reserveId pointing to one asset of one Hub, so the same underlying can be listed more than once on a single Spoke.

Supplying does not enable collateral

Only reserves flagged with setUsingAsCollateral count towards the health factor; without it every borrow reverts with HealthFactorBelowThreshold. Hence a fuse of its own for collateral.

There is no E-Mode

Aave V4 has no E-Mode: risk is segmented by choosing the Spoke. One market, ID 49, covers every Spoke.

For example, the Bluechip Spoke lists USDC twice: as reserve 4 (Prime hub) and reserve 7 (Core hub), with different rates and caps.

Contracts

Four fuses and a library.

All stateless (VERSION and MARKET_ID immutables only), run by delegatecall from PlasmaVault.execute, each with enterTransient and exitTransient variants for batched flows.

ActionAaveV4SupplyFuseSupply and withdraw on a reserve, plus the instant withdraw path for user redemptions. ActionAaveV4BorrowFuseBorrow and repay on a reserve. ActionAaveV4CollateralFuseEnable or disable a reserve as the vault's collateral. BalanceAaveV4BalanceFuseNAV: the sum of (supplied − total debt) × price over the granted reserves, in USD (WAD). LibraryAaveV4SubstrateLibEncodes and decodes reserve substrates and checks permissions. Interfaceext/IAaveV4SpokeA subset of Aave's real ISpoke, its struct layouts identical.
Permissions

One word says what the vault may do.

Each grant is a bytes32 substrate: a Spoke, a reserve and two flags. Any grant allows supply, withdraw, repay and disabling collateral; isCollateral adds enabling collateral; canBorrow adds borrowing.

The substrate

getReserveGrant probes the four flag variants of a (spoke, reserveId) pair and returns their union, so the result does not depend on the order of granted substrates. Atomists are expected to grant one variant per reserve.

Actions

What each fuse checks before it acts.

AaveV4SupplyFuse

  • enter checks the grant, cross-checks the reserve's underlying, caps the amount at the vault's balance, supplies, then enforces minShares.
  • exit withdraws with any grant.
  • instantWithdraw serves user redemptions: only for reserves never granted as collateral and not collateral on-chain, catching a failing Spoke so one reserve cannot block every withdrawal.

    revert AaveV4SupplyFuseUnsupportedSubstrate("enter", data_.spoke, data_.reserveId);
}

ERC20(data_.asset).forceApprove(data_.spoke, finalAmount);

(uint256 shares, ) = IAaveV4Spoke(data_.spoke).supply(data_.reserveId, finalAmount, address(this));
if (shares < data_.minShares) {
    revert AaveV4SupplyFuseInsufficientShares(shares, data_.minShares);
}

AaveV4BorrowFuse

  • enter borrows only with canBorrow; the Spoke reverts HealthFactorBelowThreshold if no collateral is enabled.
  • exit repays with any grant, so debt can always be repaid even after canBorrow is revoked.
  • The repay is capped at the vault's balance; the Spoke caps it at the total debt.
AaveV4BorrowFuse.sol · exitL188
if (!AaveV4SubstrateLib.canSupply(MARKET_ID, data_.spoke, data_.reserveId)) {
    revert AaveV4BorrowFuseUnsupportedSubstrate("exit", data_.spoke, data_.reserveId);
}
_validateReserveAsset(IAaveV4Spoke(data_.spoke), data_.reserveId, data_.asset);

uint256 balance = ERC20(data_.asset).balanceOf(address(this));
uint256 repayAmount = IporMath.min(balance, data_.amount);
ERC20(data_.asset).forceApprove(data_.spoke, repayAmount);

(uint256 sharesRepaid, uint256 repaid) = IAaveV4Spoke(data_.spoke).repay(
    data_.reserveId, repayAmount, address(this)
);

AaveV4CollateralFuse

  • enter enables a reserve as collateral, only with a grant carrying isCollateral.
  • exit disables it with any grant; the Spoke enforces the health factor.
  • Needed because supplying alone never enables collateral on Aave V4.

AaveV4BalanceFuse

  • Walks the granted reserve substrates, counting each (spoke, reserveId) pair once.
  • Sums (supplied − total debt) × price per reserve through PriceOracleMiddleware, normalised to WAD.
  • Debt is always read, whatever canBorrow says, so revoking a borrow permission can never hide debt. Reverts if debt exceeds supply.

A typical flow

Supply, collateralise, borrow. One call.

Three fuse actions in one PlasmaVault.execute: the README's own example on the Bluechip Spoke.

01 · Supply fuseSupply 10 WETH

Into reserve 0 of the Bluechip Spoke.

02 · Collateral fuseEnable collateral

On the same reserve 0, so it counts towards the health factor.

03 · Borrow fuseBorrow 10,000 USDC

From reserve 4, Bluechip's USDC on the Prime hub.

For atomists

Grants and open positions.

The fuses deliberately do not tie a grant change to existing positions. grantMarketSubstrates replaces the whole set, so the atomist keeps these rules.

Situation
Effect
Rule
A reserve with debt loses its grant
Debt drops out of the balance: NAV overstated, share price inflated
Never revoke a reserve the vault borrows; repay first
A reserve with supply loses its grant
Supply drops out of NAV and cannot be withdrawn until re-granted
Withdraw first, or re-grant to recover
Collateral re-granted without isCollateral
Stays enabled on the Spoke; enabling again is refused, disabling works
Disable collateral before downgrading the grant
Debt re-granted without canBorrow
New borrows refused, repay works, debt still counted
The intended way to wind a position down
Reserve granted before Aave lists it
Ignored by the balance fuse; supply and borrow revert ReserveNotListed
Harmless: active once listed

Only the last row is tolerated in code; the others are documented risks, not enforced invariants.

  • No position manager, ever. The vault acts for itself on the Spoke (onBehalfOf == msg.sender).
  • Instant withdraw is double-guarded. Limited to non-collateral reserves by grant flag and by live on-chain status, so user withdrawals can never degrade a leveraged position.
  • Exact approvals. Only granted Spokes are called; supply and repay pull exactly the approved amount (SafeERC20.forceApprove).
  • Aave's own limits stand. Reserve paused and frozen flags and Hub caps are enforced by Aave; the fuses do not pre-check them.
