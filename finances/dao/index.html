Methodology
A Fusion vault charges a performance fee on its profit and a management fee on its assets, both paid in new shares, so its share price is what depositors keep. Each fee is split between the vault's own recipients (its curator or operator) and the IPOR DAO, at rates read on-chain from the vault's fee manager (dao-fees.json); an older vault whose fees go straight to the DAO's treasury pays the DAO all of them. This page runs Key Metrics' arithmetic over every day of every vault's history (tvl-snapshots.json): the management fee accrues each day on the day's TVL, and the performance fee on each rise of the share price to a new high, grossed up for the fees taken (a recovery after a fall is not new profit). A rise read across more than three days, a share price out of range and a move of over 5% a day earn nothing. Today's fee terms stand for the whole history. Annualized is Key Metrics' figure, the year ahead at today's TVL and APY. Built by tools/build-earnings.js (earnings-daily.json) each time the snapshots or the fee terms update.
