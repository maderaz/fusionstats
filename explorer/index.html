About this vault

Methodology
The vault list, its APY and TVL now are IPOR's (ipor-vaults.json). The history is the vault's own: its TVL and share price at a block a day (tvl-snapshots.json), and the APY drawn from the share price over the week before each day, a year's worth. Markets are read on-chain every six hours (collect-vault-markets.js): each market's balance as the vault keeps it (totalAssetsInMarket), and in Aave V3, Morpho and Euler V2 each position's supply, collateral and debt as their balance fuses read them, at the rates those markets quote. A loop's collateral (a staked token such as cbETH) earns its own yield instead: its price against the token borrowed against it, now and about a month back, a year's worth; where there is nothing to measure it by, its APY is left empty, and so is the market's net APY. Total value managed is everything in the markets, borrowed-for included; leverage is that over TVL. Historical allocation is each market's balance at the TVL snapshots' blocks, in dollars at that day's price. Capacity is the vault's supply cap (getTotalSupplyCap, in shares) in its asset. The curator and the category are read from the vault's name.
