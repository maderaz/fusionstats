How it's computed
Share price is convertToAssets(1 share) in the underlying: about 1.0 at launch, trending up with yield. Readings outside 0.1–100 are scaling errors in the snapshots and are dropped; a window left with a jump over 3× or a move over 300% is flagged ⚠. Δ is the first to the last trusted price in the window. sharePrice APY annualises it with (1 + r)^(365 / days) − 1, days being the time actually elapsed between those two snapshots, so a vault sampled several times a day does not get a 50000% APY from intra-day noise. FE APY is the IPOR app's rate, fee-adjusted. Deviation = FE APY − sharePrice APY: small means the app's rate matches reality, large (either way) means it does not. Sharp drops or sustained negatives deserve a look: a rebalance loss, a fee event, or a parse error.
