Share price = convertToAssets(1 share) in underlying terms (~1.0 at deploy, trends up with yield). Line color reflects the window Δ%; the endpoint dot signals current momentum (green = last point higher than the previous, red = lower). "Δ" = first→last sharePrice across the selected timeframe. APY annualizes that move via the compound formula (1 + r)^(365 / days) − 1 where days is the actual elapsed time (in seconds, not point count) between the first and last trusted snapshots — so vaults sampled multiple times per day don't get a bogus 50000% APY from intra-day noise. Sharp drops or sustained negatives need investigation (rebalance loss, fee event, parse error). Click column headers to sort, use the toolbar to switch timeframe or filter by chain.
