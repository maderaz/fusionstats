Fees charged · onboarding / offboarding / performance / management, each its own series

Fees are taken by minting shares to the fee recipient, so each mint is a fee event. A mint in the same transaction as a deposit is the onboarding contribution, one alongside a withdrawal is the exit fee, and standalone mints are performance and management accrual split by recipient. Amounts are in vault shares.
