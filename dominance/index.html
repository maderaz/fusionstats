How it's measured

All Morpho loans is borrowAssetsUsd summed over Morpho's listed markets on Ethereum (the 1,000 largest by borrow), and Fusion borrowed the same across the Morpho positions of the vaults listed above. The share is one over the other. Both come from the Morpho API, asked from this page and kept for 15 minutes.

The chart's history is recorded by the collector about every hour: each reading for the last 7 days, a daily average before that. It checks every vault in vaults.json, which is not quite the list above, so the newest reading and the figures can differ a little.
